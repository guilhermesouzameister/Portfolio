The craft

  1. Reference & brief

    I start from the brand’s visual DNA: a study of its digital personality and the chosen product, taken from the label PNG file or a custom bottle.

  2. Geometry & material

    Bottles are built to real manufacturing specifications: wall thickness, glass taper, base profile and neck finish. Materials are physically based, with glass at measured IOR values, liquids with accurate absorption, and label substrates with scanned texture data.

  3. Lighting & lookdev

    Each scene uses a calibrated studio rig that mirrors professional product photography: soft key lights, precise rim highlights and controlled fill ratios. Environment maps and area lights are tuned for exact caustics and specular reflections.

  4. Render & delivery

    Final renders come from Blender Cycles at high sample counts with adaptive sampling. Each deliverable fits its platform: WebP for web, ProRes for video, with full EXR masters archived for re-rendering. A 3D Performance Blueprint accompanies every delivery.
